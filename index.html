🛡️ Why Defensive Publication Matters

Defensive publication is a proven legal strategy used by major corporations for decades — IBM, Google, and Microsoft all use it. But until Nano Banana, there was no free, accessible tool for independent inventors and small innovators to do the same.

When a patent office examines a patent application, it searches for prior art — any public document that describes the invention before the patent was filed. If prior art exists, the patent cannot be granted. Nano Banana creates that prior art record for you, instantly and for free.

This is especially critical in India and other developing nations, where millions of innovative ideas go unprotected simply because inventors cannot afford the ₹15,000–₹1,50,000+ cost of filing a patent.
